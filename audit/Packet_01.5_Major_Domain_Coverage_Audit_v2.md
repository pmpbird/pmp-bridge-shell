This audit reassesses the full discovery map after Passes 46–54. It does not validate individual records, deduplicate candidates, route work, close discovery, or claim practical saturation.

## Method

A domain is:

- **DEDICATED** when at least one pass directly searches the domain from its own operating reality, dependencies, affected people, failure modes, and long-term consequences;
- **PARTIAL** when meaningful coverage exists but the domain remains fragmented across other passes;
- **MISSING** when no pass provides a sufficiently direct broad-domain search.

Dedicated coverage means that the family has been searched directly. It does not mean that every risk in that family has been found.

#### PARTIAL-V2-01 — Energy production, storage, and fuel systems

Grid delivery, environmental energy use, mining, nuclear systems, hardware, and critical infrastructure are covered. However, fossil-fuel extraction and processing, refineries, pipelines, hydrogen, battery storage, renewable generation, grid-forming resources, fuel terminals, and decommissioning remain fragmented.

WHY STILL PARTIAL: no pass searches the full energy-production and storage chain as one interacting system.

#### PARTIAL-V2-02 — Chemical and high-hazard process industries

Laboratories, hazardous materials, mining, manufacturing, waste, contamination, operational technology, and emergency response are covered. Continuous-process plants, refineries, chemical manufacturing, pressure systems, runaway reactions, flare systems, process isolation, management of change, and process-safety culture remain fragmented.

WHY STILL PARTIAL: no pass searches high-hazard process safety from design through operation, maintenance, emergency shutdown, and decommissioning.

#### PARTIAL-V2-03 — Pharmaceutical and medical-product lifecycle

Pass 47 covers prescribing, dispensing, medication reconciliation, interactions, shortages, counterfeit medicines, and pharmacovigilance. Research, biotechnology, manufacturing, cold chain, and product liability are also covered elsewhere.

WHY STILL PARTIAL: discovery, formulation, clinical development, manufacturing scale-up, regulatory approval, device lifecycle, recall, post-market field correction, and end-of-life remain split across several passes.

#### PARTIAL-V2-04 — Natural hazards and disaster-management systems

Wildfire and emergency operations now have dedicated passes. Construction, infrastructure, transport, housing, public health, humanitarian response, and recovery are also covered.

WHY STILL PARTIAL: earthquake, tsunami, river flood, severe storm, heat, drought, volcanic, landslide, dam failure, sheltering, mass evacuation, warning governance, and long-term disaster recovery are not searched together.

#### PARTIAL-V2-05 — Rail systems and mass-freight corridors

Transportation, transit, logistics, hazardous cargo, critical infrastructure, maintenance, and emergency response are covered.

WHY STILL PARTIAL: signaling, dispatch, train integrity, grade crossings, track and rolling-stock maintenance, dangerous-goods trains, passenger evacuation, corridor communities, and accident investigation lack a dedicated rail pass.

## Fresh omitted-major-family search

The v2 audit also tested for broad real-world systems absent from the v1 map. Four additional major families remain without dedicated coverage.

### MISSING-V2-01 — Freshwater, watersheds, dams, groundwater, and water-resource governance

Water utilities, oceans, agriculture, mining, sanitation, critical infrastructure, and climate effects are covered separately.

MISSING SCOPE:

- watershed-scale allocation and cumulative withdrawal
- dams, reservoirs, levees, and controlled releases
- groundwater depletion, recharge, subsidence, and contamination
- drought allocation and water rights
- upstream/downstream governance
- freshwater ecosystems and environmental flows
- flood-control tradeoffs and dam-failure consequences

WHY MAJOR: freshwater connects drinking water, food, energy, ecosystems, settlement, industry, and conflict across jurisdictional boundaries.

### MISSING-V2-02 — Mental-health, behavioral-health, and addiction-service systems

Crisis, cognition, stress, health, medication, employment, family, custody, and emergency response contain related records.

MISSING SCOPE:

- outpatient and inpatient behavioral-health systems
- involuntary treatment and capacity decisions
- continuity between crisis, hospital, community, and housing
- addiction treatment, withdrawal, relapse, and harm reduction
- therapeutic records and confidentiality
- workforce shortages and waitlists
- peer support, cultural safety, and long-term recovery

WHY MAJOR: fragments exist, but no pass searches the complete service system from prevention through crisis and sustained recovery.

### MISSING-V2-03 — Social care, long-term care, aging, and disability-support systems

Family caregiving, guardianship, healthcare, housing, employment, benefits, accessibility, and emergency response are covered.

MISSING SCOPE:

- home- and community-based services
- long-term-care facilities and congregate living
- personal assistance and direct-care workforce
- elder abuse, neglect, isolation, and financial exploitation
- disability-service eligibility and continuity
- respite, caregiver collapse, and substitute decision-making
- transition between hospital, home, housing, and institutional care

WHY MAJOR: support failure can remove autonomy, housing, health, communication, and survival even when each adjacent system appears functional.

### MISSING-V2-04 — Military, defense, weapons lifecycle, and veteran-transition systems

Conflict, civilian protection, sanctions, space, manufacturing, cybersecurity, hazardous materials, and public governance are covered.

MISSING SCOPE:

- defense procurement and contractor dependence
- command authority and escalation control
- weapons storage, transport, maintenance, and decommissioning
- training-range and unexploded-ordnance stewardship
- autonomous and dual-use defense systems
- service-member health, moral injury, and family burden
- demobilization, veteran healthcare, benefits, and civilian transition

WHY MAJOR: humanitarian consequences are covered, but the institutions and material lifecycle that generate, control, store, and retire military capability are not directly searched.
