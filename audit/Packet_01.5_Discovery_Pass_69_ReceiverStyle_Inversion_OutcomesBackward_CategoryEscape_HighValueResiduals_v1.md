## Receiver-style inversion method

The challenge started from outcomes such as:

- a person is harmed although every participating system followed its own rule;
- a service reports success although the protected function is gone;
- correction is completed although the consequence remains;
- evidence exists although no institution can combine it;
- responsibility exists although no actor has sufficient control;
- recovery is declared although the original population or environment cannot return.

Candidates already represented by prior passes were rejected as semantic repeats.
