## Governing distinction

A **major root-system family** is a broad system whose operating reality introduces distinct classes of dependency, authority, physical harm, information failure, continuity failure, or long-term stewardship.

A **sector-specific subdomain** applies those root structures inside a narrower industry or service. A subdomain can still require a dedicated future pass when PMP Current enters that workflow, but an unlimited list of possible industries cannot serve as the stopping rule for broad-domain discovery.

Therefore:

- missing major root-system families block cross-domain saturation testing;
- materially partial major root-system families block cross-domain saturation testing;
- conditionally relevant sector subdomains go onto the reopening watch list and do not automatically block the next phase.
